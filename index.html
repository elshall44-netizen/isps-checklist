ISPS Checklist Reviewer

Ship Security Plan Review

MSC-CKS-00009 · Rev 3
SSP → Substantive Cross-Referenced Review

Read the plan. Judge every requirement.

Drop in the vessel's Ship Security Plan — the blank MSC-CKS-00009 checklist is built into the app; the SSA, the Flag State Special Instructions (FSSI) and the CSP / other requirement documents are optional uploads that sharpen the review. The reviewer maps the plan's structure — chapters, sections, annexes — then goes item by item through the checklist. Section 1 items are referenced from the SSA, Section 2 items from the SSP. It returns the checklist with Yes / No / N / A ticked, section/page references filled, a documented basis for every "No", and a short summary of gaps and observations.

Analysis engine

Standalone · meaning & chapter matching

Everything runs inside your browser — no account, no API, no billing, and the plan never leaves this device. This is a desktop assessment prior to the initial audit: every checklist item is recorded as Yes (evidence located, real section & page cited), Partially Addressed (located but the reviewer must verify implementation), Not Evident (no provision located — amendment or supporting evidence required), No (conflicts with the requirement) or N/A. Document control, language and revision status are checked automatically, and the summary states a suggested review outcome. Matching is meaning-oriented: maritime-security synonyms are recognised (CCTV ↔ surveillance, DoS ↔ Declaration of Security, gangway ↔ accommodation ladder…), every item is anchored to the exact chapter that best fits the requirement and judged inside it, and level-specific items are checked for genuine security level 1 / 2 / 3 context at the cited chapter. The result is a documented draft — the reviewer confirms substance against the plan and signs.
